# Mosque_01 interior lighting - investigation report (2026-10-25)

Legend: [V] verified in docs/data, [I] inference (not confirmed by docs; test it).

## What the docs actually give (limited)
The MCP wiki search returns heavily truncated pages. Verified [V] from the BIKI "EBT: Portal Tools" page:
- Portals/volumes are normally generated by EBT from sockets; the tool puts planes at "the right transformations, normals, naming and material". Material depends on portal size; a frame "slightly bigger than the norm" gets no portal material and needs a manual one. Margin for error is small and every portal must be checked.
- PRTVOL volumes use the dummyVolume material and "resize to the room size". Collider needs layer preset BuildingFireView (our prefab RigidBody has it).
- Model QA warns about missing Probe Volume / BSP / Portal / sockets. The audio page confirms rooms come from the light-portal system (GRoomSize, GInterior).
- No page text I could reach states portal normal direction, overlap rules, or probe re-capture UI. Those are [I] below. The addon copy of ProbeHouse_Base.et is only a stub (it references its own base GUID, no settings visible), so its capture settings could not be read. The church .et copies carry no probe/portal data (that lives in the church xob.meta/FBX, not present in the addon), so the comparison is limited to the prefab level: the church has NO separate probe child in ChurchSmall_E_01.et, so probe placement there is baked into its model/world, not copied.

## Our data (all [V])
- xob.meta: 8 VolumeParams BOXVOL_01..08, all Lerp 100, Ev 0, FarEvBias 0 (defaults, never tuned). Portal emats are `MatLightPortal {}` with metas. dummyvolume assigned.
- Hall: PRTVOL_01 / BSP_01 / BOXVOL_01-04: octagon apothem 8.35, Z 0.95..5.58. Dome: PRTVOL_02 / BSP_02 / BOXVOL_05-08: apothem 4.4, Z 5.63..13.0.
- Portals: 35 windows, planes (w+0.15) x (h+0.15): body 1.35x2.20, drum 0.90x1.25; materials 130x182 ARCH / 70x142. Normal flipped to point to building centre (inward).
- Prefab: one ProbeHouse_Base at (0, 2.8, 0); carpets at y 0.945 (hall) and 0.96 (porch, y=11).

## Findings, most likely cause first
1. **Carpets sit below the volumes [V data, I effect].** Carpet origin y=0.945 < volume floor 0.95 (hall floor itself is 0.93). Dynamic props pick probe/room data by their position; being 5 mm outside every BOXVOL/PRTVOL they get no interior probe while the camera is "inside" -> black, but fine from outside. Porch carpet (y=11, outside the hall volumes) is the same class of issue. Fix: lower hall volumes/BSP floor to ~0.85 (below floor; harmless), keep carpets at 0.945+. Quick test without Blender: raise carpets to y=1.00 in the prefab (but then they float 7 cm; use only as a diagnostic).
2. **Dome/drum volumes do not contain the dome [V data].** Drum opening radius is 4.96 m but PRTVOL_02/BOXVOL_05-08/BSP_02 are an octagon of apothem 4.4 (circumradius 4.76) prism up to 13 m; drum windows and drum wall are outside it, the dome shell widens beyond it low down and tapers inside above. Surfaces outside every volume get no probe -> black dome. Fix: apothem >= 5.1 for the drum part (cover the circle r 4.96 + wall), and a tapered/stepped set for the dome or accept slight overshoot (dome top above a few m can be 2 stacked prisms of decreasing apothem). Overshooting through the thin roof is the lesser evil compared with black.
3. **Hall and dome rooms are disconnected [V data, I effect].** Hall top 5.58, dome bottom 5.63 (gap 5 cm) and the ceiling opening (r 4.96) is wider than the dome octagon (4.4). Overlap them: extend PRTVOL_02/BOXVOL_05-08 down to ~5.0 (inside the hall volume) so the volumes intersect/touch. Overlapping BOXVOLs are the normal way to make blends; overlapping BSP caused a build failure per our notes (keep BSP non-overlapping: make BSP_02 start exactly at BSP_01's top, no gap, or make one BSP).
4. **Probe covers only the hall [V data].** One probe at y 2.8; dome volumes (4 BOXVOLs) contain no probe. [I] volumes only define where a probe's influence applies; dome volumes need a probe inside them. Add a second GameEnvironmentProbeEntity (ProbeHouse_Base) at about (0, 9.5, 0) (inside drum/dome), keep hall probe at (0, 2.8, 0). Check in World Editor that each probe's box overlaps only its own BOXVOL set.
5. **Probes were never re-captured [I, strongly suggested by Plan M6 note].** Interior lighting is only correct after the probe is regenerated with the final FBX; a probe captured before volumes/portals existed contains open sky = too bright, evenly lit walls. Manual step (exact UI name not confirmed in docs): in World Editor open a world with Mosque_01 placed, select the GameEnvironmentProbeEntity, in the Attributes/context menu use the probe's capture/generate (a "Generate/Update probe" style button), or run the Environment probes regeneration from the Plugins/World menu; then save. Do this in a world instance, not only in prefab edit mode, with the sun/world light entity present.
6. **Portal geometry vs windows [V data, I effect].** Plan said window opening z 1.9-4.8 (about 2.9 m tall) but the body portal is 2.20 m tall and material 130x182 (1.82); the pane max-z-based height (2.05) may be smaller than the real opening, so only the lower part of the window admits light. Draw the portal to cover the true glass opening (measure from the wall hole, not the pane object). EBT normally scales planes to the frame; the wiki warns of "no scale/huge scale" planes. Also verify the 0.15 margin: the standard 130x182 plane is 1.30x1.82; ours are non-standard sizes with a standard material, [I] the engine may use the plane geometry, not the material size, so it is probably fine, but test by setting exactly the standard size on one window.
7. **Portal normals [I].** Ours point to the building centre (EBT convention per our notes, unverified in docs). A flipped plane would make light enter from the wrong side (dark interior, bright exterior-facing). Cheap test: flip normals on 2 windows on one side and compare in game. Also the entrance portal (door) has no standard material (195x182 emat exists, but the measured size differed); assign the closest standard material manually.
8. **Sun light only through portals [I].** Glass is vanilla window_glass_uneven_EXT; the window prefab replaces the pane mesh, so no extra light path. Brightness inside depends on portal + probe, not on glass.
9. **Too bright / evenly bright walls [I].** Volume params are defaults (Lerp 100, Ev 0). After recapture, if still too bright lower BOXVOL Ev (e.g. -1 to -2) and FarEvBias in the xob.meta VolumeParams per volume (hall vs dome separately); keep Lerp at 100 for fully enclosed rooms.
